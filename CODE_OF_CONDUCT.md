# Contributor Code of Conduct

> This covers how we work together on **this repository**. The rules for members of the live portal are in the app at `/conduct.php`.

## Our pledge

We — students, alumni, faculty and anyone else contributing — pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, caste, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Encouraged**

- Being welcoming to first-time contributors and juniors
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users
- Respecting the privacy of the people whose data the portal holds

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information (including data seen while testing)
- Using access to the project to view or export real users' data without a need
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Responsibilities & enforcement

Maintainers clarify standards and take fair corrective action — editing or removing comments, commits or issues, or temporarily/permanently banning contributors.

Report problems to **shiroonigami23@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

| Impact | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Minor, first time | Private written warning |
| Repeated or moderate | Temporary ban from interaction |
| Severe (harassment, data misuse) | Permanent ban |
